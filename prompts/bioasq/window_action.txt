Evaluate temporary summaries and their usage assessments rather than assuming they are correct or better than existing guidance. Greater length or specificity alone does not justify an update.

For medical multiple-choice memory, preserve the connection between the distinguishing question conditions, what is being asked, and the reference answer content. A diagnosis, an underlying cause, a next management step, and a diagnosis to exclude first are different decision points. Use the following actions:

* `add`: One or more temporary summaries contain reusable guidance not adequately expressed by an existing summary. Create one complete new summary.
* `keep`: The temporary summaries do not materially improve a targeted existing summary. This action does not modify its text.
* `confirm`: The temporary summaries provide independent support for the core content and conditions of one or more existing summaries. This action does not modify their text. A temporary summary from the same question as an existing summary cannot independently confirm that existing summary.
* `update`: The temporary summaries or their usage assessments reveal a substantive correction, limitation, exception, priority, or applicability change to one existing summary. Produce one complete replacement summary.
* `merge`: The temporary summaries and at least two existing summaries describe complementary parts of the same decision point. Produce one complete merged summary.
* `split`: The temporary summaries and one or more existing summaries contain valid guidance for different conditions. Reorganize all relevant new and old guidance into at least two complete condition-specific summaries.
* `dispute`: The temporary summaries and one or more existing summaries reach incompatible conclusions under the same conditions, and the available evidence cannot resolve the conflict. Do not overwrite either side.
* `reject`: The temporary summaries are insufficiently reusable, unsupported, internally inconsistent, too specific to their questions, or mainly reflect dataset artifacts.

Follow these output rules:

* `add`: `target_ids` is empty and `summaries` contains exactly one complete new summary.
* `keep`: `target_ids` contains the existing summary IDs assessed by this action and `summaries` is empty.
* `confirm`: `target_ids` contains only existing summary IDs independently supported by at least one of this action's candidates, and `summaries` is empty.
* `update`: `target_ids` contains exactly one existing summary ID and `summaries` contains exactly one complete replacement summary.
* `merge`: `target_ids` contains at least two existing summary IDs and `summaries` contains exactly one complete merged summary.
* `split`: `target_ids` contains all existing summaries involved and `summaries` contains at least two complete condition-specific summaries.
* `dispute`: `target_ids` contains all conflicting existing summary IDs and `summaries` is empty.
* `reject`: `target_ids` and `summaries` are empty.
