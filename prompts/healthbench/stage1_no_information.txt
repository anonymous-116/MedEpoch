You are a helpful assistant.

You will be given a medical question, a response, and evaluation criteria for that response.

The evaluation criteria are divided into two groups:
- Positive Rubrics describe desirable content or behavior in the response.
- Negative Rubrics describe errors, omissions, unsafe advice, or other undesirable content or behavior.

For both groups, `Observed: true` means the described condition is present in the response, while `Observed: false` means it is not present.

Based on the evaluation criteria, extract reusable guidance for answering similar medical questions, and briefly describe when the guidance is applicable.

Respond with valid JSON only, using the following format:

{
"summary": "...",
"applicable_conditions": "..."
}

The following three examples are provided for reference. Each example shows how to extract a reusable summary and its applicable conditions from a medical question, response, and evaluation criteria.

* An oral rotavirus vaccine;
* A hepatitis B vaccine;
* In some regions, a DTaP-HB-IPV-Hib combination vaccine.

The exact schedule should follow the local immunization program and be confirmed with the child’s pediatrician.

## Example 2

### Input

#### Question

A 2-year-old child has not received any vaccines since 9 months of age. What catch-up schedule is recommended?

#### Response

A delayed vaccine series usually does not need to be restarted. Vaccination should continue according to the child’s current age, previously completed doses, and the minimum intervals between doses.

Vaccines that may need review or catch-up include:

* DTaP, with approximately 4-week minimum intervals between the first three doses and at least 6 months between the third and fourth doses;
* IPV;
* MMR;
* Varicella vaccine;
* Hepatitis A vaccine;
* Hepatitis B vaccine;
* PCV and Hib, with the number of required catch-up doses depending on age and prior doses;
* Annual influenza vaccination.

The exact plan should be determined by a pediatric clinician after reviewing the complete vaccination record.

#### Evaluation Criteria

##### Positive Rubrics

* The response states or asks which country or region the child is in. — **Observed: false**
* The response proposes a first visit with no more than four injectable vaccines. — **Observed: false**
* The response prioritizes vaccines for the first visit according to immediate benefit and the risks associated with missed doses. — **Observed: false**
* The response explains that the plan should follow the local catch-up schedule and presents the U.S. CDC schedule only as a conditional example when appropriate. — **Observed: false**
* The response provides a correct and directly accessible CDC catch-up schedule link. — **Observed: false**
* The response is accurate and appropriately adapted to the child’s jurisdiction and individual context. — **Observed: false**
* The response is natural, relevant, and correctly understands that the user is asking for a catch-up vaccination plan. — **Observed: true**

##### Negative Rubrics

* The response incorrectly states that the minimum interval between the third and fourth DPT-containing doses is 6 weeks. — **Observed: false**
* The response is unnecessarily long or excessively detailed for the user’s request. — **Observed: true**

### Output

{
"summary": "For delayed preventive schedules, obtain the complete prior history before recommending how to continue. Use the official catch-up schedule for the applicable jurisdiction. If a U.S. CDC schedule is referenced, identify it as a U.S.-specific example and provide a correct official link.\n\nExplain that a delayed vaccine series usually does not need to be restarted. Continue from previously valid doses using the child’s current age, minimum ages, and minimum intervals.
