##### Information 1

In patients with a history of bulimia nervosa presenting with chronic watery diarrhea, hypokalemia, and metabolic alkalosis, suspect laxative or diuretic abuse rather than secretory tumors like VIPoma. The key differentiating endoscopic finding is melanosis coli (dark pigmentation of the colonic mucosa), which is pathognomonic for chronic anthraquinone laxative use. While VIPoma causes watery diarrhea and hypokalemia, it is typically associated with metabolic acidosis due to bicarbonate loss, whereas the metabolic alkalosis seen here, combined with the specific endoscopic appearance and psychiatric history, strongly supports medication abuse.

In differentiating factitious disorder from somatic symptom disorder or organic pathology, look for objective evidence of self-inflicted injury or manipulation, such as "crusts along the course of veins" (indicating self-injection or trauma) or unexplained laboratory abnormalities inconsistent with the stated history. While patients with somatic symptom disorder experience genuine distress over symptoms, they do not intentionally produce or feign them; the presence of physical signs of self-harm or deliberate symptom induction (e.g., requesting invasive procedures despite negative workups) is the key discriminator for factitious disorder.
